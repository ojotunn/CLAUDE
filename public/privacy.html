Privacy

What Claudeploy keeps.

Stored

When Claude prepares a launch or a buy, the server stores the launch parameters you gave (name, ticker, description, links, tax settings, dev buy amount), the wallet address that connects on the signing page, the unsigned transaction it built, and the transaction hash your wallet returns. Once the transaction confirms, the token and curve addresses are stored and listed publicly on the Tokens page together with the deployer address, because they are already public on the chain.

Not stored

Private keys, seed phrases, signatures, your Claude account, your conversations. The server never receives them. The signing page talks to your wallet extension directly.

Logs

The server keeps standard request logs (IP address, path, time) for rate limiting and debugging, rotated by the hosting provider. There are no analytics scripts, no advertising trackers and no cookies set by Claudeploy.

Third parties

Previews and status checks query the public Robinhood Chain RPC. Signing links may be sent to you by Claude, which is operated by Anthropic under its own privacy policy. pons stores launch metadata on-chain under its own terms.

Removal

Prepared launches that were never signed expire after 24 hours and can be deleted on request. On-chain data cannot be removed by anyone.
