How it works

1. Say it

Name, ticker, a line about it, and how much ETH you want in as the first buyer. Claude turns that into a launch and simulates it on-chain.

2. Check it

You see the supply, the fee, your dev buy and its share of supply, the total cost and any warnings. You confirm, Claude hands you a link.

3. Sign it

Connect your wallet, see the exact contract address before signing, sign once. Launch and dev buy settle in the same transaction.

Your keys stay yours.

Claudeploy is a builder, not a custodian. It prepares a transaction, shows you what it will do, and hands it to your wallet. Signing and broadcasting happen on your side, every time.

  • Non-custodial. No seed phrase, no private key, no deposit. Only your address, and only after you connect.
  • Simulated first. Every launch is dry-run from your wallet before you are asked to sign. The address on the page is the address you get.
  • One transaction. Launch and dev buy go through the pons launch-and-buy router together. No window for a sniper between the two.
  • Terms pinned. The pons terms you were quoted are locked into the transaction. If pons changes them, the launch reverts instead of repricing.
  • Dev buy capped at 5% of supply. Larger requests are reduced automatically and you are told.
