How it works

1. Say it

Name, ticker, a line about it, and how much {{UNIT}} you want in as the first buyer{{#argus}}, plus the tax and how it is split{{/argus}}. Claude turns that into a launch and simulates it on-chain.

2. Check it

You see the supply, {{#pons}}the fee, {{/pons}}{{#argus}}the tax and split, {{/argus}}your dev buy and its share of supply, the total cost and any warnings. You confirm, Claude hands you a link.

3. Sign it

Connect your wallet, see the exact contract address before signing, sign. Launch and dev buy settle in the same transaction.

Your keys stay yours.

{{APP}} is a builder, not a custodian. It prepares a transaction, shows you what it will do, and hands it to your wallet. Signing and broadcasting happen on your side, every time.

  • Non-custodial. No seed phrase, no private key, no deposit. Only your address, and only after you connect.
  • Simulated first. Every launch is dry-run from your wallet before you are asked to sign. The address on the page is the address you get.
    • {{#pons}}
  • One transaction. Launch and dev buy go through the pons launch-and-buy router together. No window for a sniper between the two.
  • Terms pinned. The pons terms you were quoted are locked into the transaction. If pons changes them, the launch reverts instead of repricing.
    • {{/pons}} {{#argus}}
  • One transaction. Token, tax hook, Uniswap v4 pool and your dev buy are created together by the Argus portal. No window for a sniper between launch and first buy.
  • Tax fixed forever. Buy tax, sell tax and the split are written at launch and cannot be changed by anyone, including you.
  • Hook mined locally. Argus needs a hook address with specific bits; {{APP}} mines it for your wallet in a fraction of a second and predicts the token address the same way the portal does.
    • {{/argus}}
  • Dev buy capped at 5% of supply. Larger requests are reduced automatically and you are told.
The agent

Say "launch it with an agent". The agent gets a wallet of its own; you send it the launch money on the signing page; it launches the token and is its creator on Argus. From then on it collects the creator share of the tax, buys itself back and burns, airdrops recent buyers, pays you a salary if you want one, keeps a gas reserve, and posts about it. No approvals, no caps, no dashboard to babysit.

Posts appear on the agent's public page with a copy button. Connect your own X API keys and it posts from your account by itself.
