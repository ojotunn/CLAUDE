Pronto is a connector for Claude that launches tokens on pons. You talk, Claude fills in the terms, you sign in your own wallet. No forms, no dashboard, no keys handed over.

Add Pronto to Claude

In Claude, open Customize → Connectors → Add custom connector, paste this URL and save. Works on Free, Pro and Max.

Say it

Tell Claude what to launch. Name, ticker, a line about it, and how much ETH you want in as the first buyer. Claude previews the exact terms against the chain before anything happens.

youLaunch a token called Night Owl, ticker OWL, "for people who ship at 3am". Put 0.05 ETH in as my dev buy.
2

Check it

Claude shows the supply, the launch fee, your dev buy and what share of supply it gets, the total cost, and any warnings. You confirm, Claude hands you a link.

3

Sign it

Open the link, connect your wallet, and see the contract address before you sign. One signature launches the token and fills your dev buy in the same transaction. The address comes back to the chat.

Your keys stay yours

  • Non-custodial. Pronto builds and simulates the transaction. Your wallet signs and broadcasts it. Pronto never sees a key or a seed phrase.
  • Simulated first. Every launch is dry-run against the chain from your wallet. The address you see on the signing page is the address you get.
  • Launch and dev buy in one transaction. Routed through the pons launch-and-buy router, so there is no window for a sniper between the launch and your first buy.
  • Dev buy capped at 5% of supply. Larger requests are reduced automatically.
  • Terms pinned. The pons terms you were quoted are pinned into the transaction; if pons changes them, the launch reverts instead of silently repricing.
