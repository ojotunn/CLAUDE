Why does the page ask for two or three signatures?

With a dev buy, the launch needs one approval first (USDC for the Argus portal), then the launch itself. A buy through the Uniswap v4 pool needs, the first time only, an approval of USDC for Permit2 and an approval of the router on Permit2; later buys are a single signature. The page walks you through them one at a time and waits for each to confirm.

After launch

{{#pons}}
Where do the creator fees go?

To the creator fee recipient in the launch: your wallet, unless you named another address in the chat. pons holds them in its fee escrow; claiming is done on pons, not here.

Can I sell my dev buy?

Yes, on pons or any wallet that can call the bonding curve. {{APP}} only prepares launches and buys.

What is graduation?

When a curve raises 4.2 ETH, pons moves the token to a Uniswap v4 pool. From then on trades happen there. {{APP}}'s buy tool stops at graduation and points you to pons.

Can {{APP}} undo a launch?

No. Nobody can. A token on pons is a contract on Robinhood Chain; once deployed it stays deployed. That is why the preview comes first and the signature is yours.

{{/pons}} {{#argus}}
Where do the creator fees go?

Every buy and sell pays the fixed tax. Argus keeps 10% of it; the rest is split the way the launch set it: creator, buy back & burn and liquidity. The creator share is credited to the wallet that launched, in USDC, inside the token's splitter contract, and claimed on Argus (or by the agent, if the agent launched).

Can I give holders a share of the tax?

Argus has a holders share (USDC pushed to every holder pro rata, no claim), but the Argus contracts only create the dividend tracker for launchers registered by Argus. {{APP}} checks that before the preview and tells you when a wallet is not registered. Regular launches split the tax between the creator, buy back & burn and liquidity.

Can I change the tax later?

No. Buy tax, sell tax and the split are fixed at launch, forever. That is the Argus design: every trader knows the rules before the first trade.

What is the bond at $45,000?

The token opens at a $2,500 market cap in a Uniswap v4 pool that holds the whole supply. When a buy pushes the market cap past $45,000, the liquidity locks permanently, inside that same transaction. Trading continues in the same pool; nothing migrates.

Why does my agent have to launch the token?

On Argus the creator is the wallet that signed the launch, and there is no function to change it afterwards. So a {{APP}} agent, which lives off the creator share, has to be the one that launches. You send the launch money to its wallet; it launches, keeps the creator share, and pays you a salary if the rules say so. You can take its balance back at any time; it then forwards every fee it collects to you.

Can {{APP}} undo a launch?

No. Nobody can. A token on Argus is a contract on Arc; once deployed it stays deployed. That is why the preview comes first and the signature is yours.
