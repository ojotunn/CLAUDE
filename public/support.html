Support

Stuck? Start here.

Most problems are one of these. If yours is not, reach out below.

Connecting

Claude says it cannot see Claudeploy.

Make sure the connector is enabled in the current chat: open the tools menu in the conversation and switch Claudeploy on. New connectors are off in chats that were started before you added them.

The connector fails to add.

Copy the URL exactly as shown on the docs page, ending in /mcp. Team and Enterprise accounts need an organization owner to add it.

Does Claudeploy need my API key or a login?

No. There is no account. Claude talks to the server, and your wallet talks to the chain. That is all.

Signing page

"No browser wallet found."

The signing page needs an injected EVM wallet: MetaMask, Rabby, Phantom with EVM enabled, Coinbase Wallet, or similar. On a phone, open the link inside your wallet's built-in browser. WalletConnect is not supported yet.

My wallet asks to add or switch to an unknown network.

That is Robinhood Chain (chain id 4663). Approve it. The page supplies the official RPC and explorer; you can verify them on the docs page.

"This wallet needs ETH on Robinhood Chain."

The wallet you connected holds less than the launch fee plus your dev buy plus gas, on Robinhood Chain specifically. Bridge ETH to Robinhood Chain, then press Re-check balance.

The link expired.

Prepared launches are valid for 24 hours because pons terms can change. Ask Claude to prepare it again; the preview takes seconds.

"pons is only accepting launches from whitelisted wallets."

pons can restrict launches to a whitelist. When it does, only wallets on that list can launch, through Claudeploy or anywhere else. Check pons for how to get on it.

Will the contract address really be the one shown?

Yes. pons derives it from your wallet, the pinned terms and a random salt chosen when the launch was prepared. If anything that feeds the address changed, the transaction would revert rather than deploy elsewhere.

I rejected the transaction by mistake.

Nothing happened on-chain. Press Sign & launch again.

After launch

Where do the creator fees go?

To the creator fee recipient in the launch: your wallet, unless you named another address in the chat. pons holds them in its fee escrow; claiming is done on pons, not here.

Can I sell my dev buy?

Yes, on pons or any wallet that can call the bonding curve. Claudeploy only prepares launches and buys.

What is graduation?

When a curve raises 4.2 ETH, pons moves the token to a Uniswap v4 pool. From then on trades happen there. Claudeploy's buy tool stops at graduation and points you to pons.

Can Claudeploy undo a launch?

No. Nobody can. A token on pons is a contract on Robinhood Chain; once deployed it stays deployed. That is why the preview comes first and the signature is yours.

Claude reported a contract address that does not exist.

It should never do that: the status tool only returns what the chain confirmed. If it happens, please send us the launch id and the chat, and do not trust the address.
